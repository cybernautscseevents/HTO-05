# VOUCH — Demo Script

## Opening

> "What if your professional reputation existed only in other people's memories?"

Introduce Ravi.

> "Ravi has nine years of experience as an electrical technician. He's completed dozens of projects. But unlike a typical professional, much of that experience doesn't exist as a portable digital record."

Do not spend too long explaining the problem.

---

## Step 1 — Create work

Open Add Work.

Enter:

> "I installed six electrical panels at a commercial building last week."

Show AI extraction.

Say:

> "Instead of forcing Ravi through a long form, VOUCH turns his natural description into a structured work record."

---

## Step 2 — Add evidence

Attach:

- work photos
- document
- confirmation request

Say:

> "The important part is that VOUCH doesn't stop at the worker's claim. It connects the claim to evidence."

---

## Step 3 — Verify

Open verifier link.

Show:

> "Did Ravi perform this work?"

Confirm.

Say:

> "A supervisor or customer can independently confirm the record without creating a complicated account."

---

## Step 4 — Show confidence

Return to Ravi.

Show:

> Panel Installation — 87% evidence confidence

Open explanation:

- 6 work records
- 4 confirmations
- 8 photos
- 2 documents

Say:

> "This isn't a claim that Ravi is '87% skilled.' It's an explainable measure of how strongly the available evidence supports this professional claim."

This distinction is important.

---

## Step 5 — Passport

Open Public Passport.

Say:

> "Now Ravi has something he can carry with him."

Show:

- identity
- experience
- demonstrated skills
- work history
- evidence
- confirmations

---

## Step 6 — QR

Show QR.

Ask judge to scan it if appropriate.

The public passport opens on their phone.

Final line:

> "VOUCH doesn't create another marketplace for skilled workers. It gives their work a professional identity they can carry anywhere."

Closing:

> "Your work. Vouched for."

---

## Demo failure fallback

If live AI/API fails:

- use seeded structured data
- demonstrate the same UI
- explain that the extraction service is live but use fallback data for reliability

Never let an external API outage destroy the entire presentation.
